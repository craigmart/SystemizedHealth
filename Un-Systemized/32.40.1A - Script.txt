Today is day one of my relaunch.

This channel—Un-Systemized—is a behind-the-scenes laboratory. It’s mostly for my own archival purposes, and honestly, to get reps back in front of the camera.

I’ve done videos and podcasts for decades. After 30 years in clinical practice as a chiropractor, I have a wealth of information that can keep me busy for years. But I needed a space to test ideas, get comfortable with new gear, and track my daily progress so I can look back and get better.

On my main channel, Systemized Health, I’ve committed to a three-year push: one long-form video and three shorts every single week. Video number one drops today.

In the past, I always ad-libbed everything. I just winged it. But looking at modern video pacing, I decided to move to a hybrid approach—light scripting mixed with natural ad-libbing.

This video is the exact result of that process.

This morning, I sat on my porch with my dog, sipped coffee, and dictated all these thoughts into the Voice Memos app on my iPhone.

Then I put that transcript into Gemini with one instruction: clean up the rambling, trim the boring parts, and organize my thoughts into a readable outline.

No AI is writing this. AI isn't creating content or coming up with ideas. It’s simply acting as my junior editor—tightening my own spoken words for efficiency and listenability.

Now, that loose script is running on my iPhone attached to my Osmo 3 camera. I’m reading about 50% scripted cues and filling in the rest naturally.

From there, the footage gets lightly edited in LumaFusion, and for shorts, I’m practicing with Descript.

Once a video is finished, I become my own librarian. The transcript becomes the authoritative asset of that content. I study the transcript, pull out the core nuggets, and index them into a permanent knowledge system built around Zettelkasten—combining physical cards and digital tools.

That’s what this channel is all about.

While the main channel focuses on health education, systemic strategies, and coaching for people navigating health challenges, Un-Systemized is for the technical nerd stuff behind the scenes.

We’re going to talk about Zettelkasten, personal knowledge management, content production workflows, and the practical mechanics of running your life.

It’s a different audience and a different algorithm.

The main channel is for 40 to 50-year-olds rebuilding their baseline health through our Systemized Health Operating System. This channel is for the creators, builders, and technical minds who want to see how the sausage gets made.

So welcome to day one. Let's get to work.
