- **The Myth**: People think quitting a new diet or intense workout routine on Thursday means they lack discipline or willpower.
- **The Biological Reality**: Human physiology is hardwired for homeostasis and energy conservation. Shocking your biology with 5 massive changes triggers a stress response.
- **Clinical Experience**: 230,000 patient visits showed me that people who succeed long-term never overhaul their life on Monday—they change ONE micro-variable at a time.
- **Action Step**: Pick one micro-habit (like 30 seconds of incline plank or drinking 16oz of water before coffee) and lock it in for 7 days.
