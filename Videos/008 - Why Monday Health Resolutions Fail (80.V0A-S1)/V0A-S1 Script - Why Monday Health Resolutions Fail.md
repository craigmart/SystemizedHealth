### 2. Talking Points (For Audio Riffing)
- **2.1 The Myth**: People think quitting a new diet or intense workout routine on Thursday means they lack discipline or willpower.
- **2.2 The Biological Reality**: Human physiology is hardwired for homeostasis and energy conservation. Shocking your biology with 5 massive changes triggers a stress response.
- **2.3 Clinical Experience**: 230,000 patient visits showed me that people who succeed long-term never overhaul their life on Monday—they change ONE micro-variable at a time.
- **2.4 Casual Research Reference**: McEwen (1998) *N Engl J Med* showing sympathetic stress response forces emergency shutdown under sudden drastic routine changes. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9428819/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9428819/)
- **2.5 Action Step**: Pick one micro-habit (like 30 seconds of incline plank or drinking 16oz of water before coffee) and lock it in for 7 days.
