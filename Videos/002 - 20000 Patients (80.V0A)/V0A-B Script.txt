[4:00] PILLAR ONE: FUEL

Your body runs on calories. Calories are simply units of energy that your mitochondria convert into ATP, the fundamental currency of cellular energy.

You get those calories from three macro-sources: carbohydrates, protein, and fat. Carbs and protein carry four kilocalories of energy per gram. Fat carries nine.

This is exactly why your body stores excess energy as fat. It is the most highly efficient way for your biology to stockpile fuel for survival. When you start gaining weight in your midsection, your body is just efficiently storing calories.

The Action Step for Fuel: If food is your biggest struggle right now, I want you to do one thing. Push your carbohydrate intake—the bread, the sugar, the cereal—into the afternoon. Do not eat carbs in the morning.

Overnight, while you sleep, your body shifts into a fasted, fat-burning mode. When you eat a heavy carbohydrate breakfast first thing in the morning, you immediately shut off that fat-burning process. Delay the carbs, and you extend that biological fat-burning window.

[5:30] PILLAR TWO: MOVEMENT

I prefer the word "movement" over "exercise." Exercise implies going to a gym and sweating for an hour. Movement is simply maintaining the physical capacity to carry your own frame through daily life.

Patients often tell me, "Doctor, I can't exercise. I'm in too much pain."

I always tell them there is not a living person who cannot exercise. If you can only move your arm back and forth four times before it gets heavy, then your current capacity is four movements. If you do not actively use that capacity, your nervous system will stop supporting it. "Use it or lose it" isn't just an old saying. It is an absolute rule of human physiology.

The Action Step for Movement: If movement is your weakest link, your only job is to hold a plank every single day for ten to thirty seconds. That is it. A plank forces full-body engagement across your legs, core, spine, and shoulders. If you cannot get on the floor, modify it. Stand in your kitchen, put your hands on the counter at a 45-degree angle, and hold an incline plank.

[7:00] PILLAR THREE: REST

You only heal when you are sleeping. If you are dealing with a chronic health crisis and you are not sleeping, you are missing the primary biological mechanism for repair.

Roughly seventy-five percent of your daily human growth hormone is secreted while you sleep. This hormone is mission-critical for repairing tissue damage and regulating your nervous system. If you cut your sleep short, you literally rob your body of the chemical required to rebuild.

The Action Step for Rest: If sleep is your struggle, your action step is incredibly simple. Calculate the average time you normally go to bed, and go to bed exactly twenty minutes earlier. Do not overcomplicate this. Just get in bed twenty minutes sooner than you think you need to.
