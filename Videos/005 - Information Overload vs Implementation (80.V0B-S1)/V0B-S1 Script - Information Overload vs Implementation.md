### Talking Points (For Audio Riffing)
- **The Problem**: High performers aren't failing due to a lack of knowledge; they are drowning in scattered health advice without an operating system.
- **The Biological Reframe**: Knowing *what* to do is useless if your nervous system is too fatigued to execute it. Information without biological sequencing creates execution friction.
- **Clinical Scenario**: Patient comes into the clinic with 15 different supplement bottles and 3 workout apps, but can't touch their toes or sleep 6 hours straight.
- **Casual Research Reference**: Marcora et al. (2009) *J Appl Physiol* showing cognitive overload inhibits physical motor plan execution in the brain. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19131473/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19131473/)
- **The Takeaway**: Stop adding new hacks. Pick one foundational baseline habit—Fuel, Move, or Rest—and automate it before adding anything else.
