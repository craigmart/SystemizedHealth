- **The Problem**: People mistake a simple neurological/somatic glitch for permanent aging or degeneration.

- **Clinical Example**: A patient complaining of tight hamstrings for 5 years—it wasn't short muscles, it was an inhibited glute motor pattern caused by sitting at a desk 10 hours a day.
- **Casual Research Reference**: Tsao et al. (2008) *Brain* showing motor cortex reorganization causes motor map errors. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18669505/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18669505/)
