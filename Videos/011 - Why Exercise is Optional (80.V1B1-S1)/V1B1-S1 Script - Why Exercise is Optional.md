### Talking Points (For Audio Riffing)
- **The Relief**: People feel guilty when they can't force themselves to go to the gym 5 days a week. Ditch the guilt.
- **Biological Fact**: Human physiology is designed for high-frequency, low-load somatic movement throughout the day, not 1 hour of intense gym suffering followed by 10 hours of desk sitting.
- **Clinical Insight**: Sitting motionless all day and then doing heavy squats at 6 PM is a recipe for spinal disc herniation.
- **Casual Research Reference**: Pontzer et al. (2012) *PLoS One* showing hunter-gatherers rarely engage in cardio sessions, but move low-intensity all day. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22848382/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22848382/)
- **Action Step**: Integrate movement micro-breaks into daily life (standing calf raises, desk planks, walking while talking on the phone).
