### Talking Points (For Audio Riffing)
- **The Puzzle**: Why does a tiny, light movement (picking up a sock or pencil) trigger a massive, excruciating muscle spasm in the lower back?
- **Neurological Mechanism**: Cortical Smudging. The brain has a high-definition motor map (homunculus) of your spine. If you don't move through full ranges daily, the map blurs like a smudged mirror.
- **The Threat Response**: When you make a surprise movement, the blurred brain map freaks out because it can't sense precise joint position, triggering a protective muscle lock-up.
- **Casual Research Reference**: Moseley & Flor (2012) *Neurorehabil Neural Repair* showing lack of varied movement blurs cortical representations. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22331213/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22331213/)
- **Action Step**: Sharp sensory feedback resets the map—do slow, controlled cat-camels or tactile spinal tracing daily.
